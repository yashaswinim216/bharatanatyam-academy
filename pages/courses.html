Our Bharatanatyam Courses

Discover the beauty of classical dance through rhythm, expression, discipline and tradition.

At Kala Bharati Bharatanatyam Academy, students can explore Bharatanatyam through progressive learning levels. Each level focuses on developing technique, confidence, artistic expression and an appreciation of Indian culture.

Level 1

Beginners

An introduction to Bharatanatyam for students who are starting their classical dance journey.

What you will learn

  • Basic body postures and movements
  • Introduction to hand gestures (mudras)
  • Basic rhythm and coordination
  • Foundational dance practice

Level 2

Junior Level

Build on the basics and develop stronger technique, rhythm, coordination and expressive movement.

What you will learn

  • Adavus and movement combinations
  • Improved rhythm and footwork
  • Facial expressions and storytelling
  • Short dance sequences
Enquire About This Course
Level 3

Senior Level

Strengthen your artistic skills through more advanced technique, expressive storytelling and choreography.

What you will learn

  • Advanced dance combinations
  • Abhinaya and expressive storytelling
  • Greater control of rhythm and movement
  • Performance practice and choreography

Advanced

Vidvat Level

A higher-level learning pathway for students seeking deeper study, artistic refinement and performance preparation.

What you will explore

  • Refinement of classical dance technique
  • Detailed expressive interpretation
  • Repertoire and choreography development
  • Advanced performance preparation
